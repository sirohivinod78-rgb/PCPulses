## PclZip

File: `src/class-pclzip-archive.php`.

The instance-scoped append and compression routines are adapted from PclZip
2.8.2 by Vincent Blavet, distributed under the GNU LGPL. The plugin extends
WordPress's installed PclZip library and does not bundle a separate full copy.
Simply Static modified these routines in 2026 to use private scratch files
without changing PclZip's global temporary-directory configuration.

Upstream source and copyright/license notice:

* https://github.com/WordPress/WordPress/blob/master/wp-admin/includes/class-pclzip.php
